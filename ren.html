02 // DEVELOPMENT

Human-oriented robotics

R.E.N. was developed in France as part of Aurelia Industries' humanoid robotics program. The unit was designed to assist human personnel across a variety of professional environments.

During its operational life, R.E.N. was deployed primarily in logistics, stock management, technical maintenance and technical assistance.

Over time, the unit demonstrated an increasing capacity for communication, observation of human behavior and adaptation to social interactions.

Unlike a conventional automated system, R.E.N. was designed to work alongside people, learn from its environment and progressively adapt to the individuals around it.
